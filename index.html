About

From the frontline to the Power Platform.

I’m a Power Platform Developer in the UK public sector, where I own Power Platform solutions end to end — from discovery and design through to deployment, governance and continuous improvement. That means real, hands-on work across the whole platform, not one corner of it: canvas and model-driven Power Apps, cloud and desktop Power Automate flows, Power BI models and reports, Dataverse data design and security, and the environment, DLP and ALM administration underneath.

My background in law enforcement and community safety sets my work apart. I spent years on the frontline of the services I now build technology for — most recently leading a team of enforcement officers — and completed an MSc in Software Engineering alongside the day job. So I understand the operational pressures, the compliance requirements, and what it actually takes for a solution to work in the public sector.

Beyond the Power Platform I also build for the web and for iOS (Swift and SwiftUI), and I care about the engineering side of low-code: proper ALM, CI/CD through Azure DevOps, and solutions the next person can maintain.
