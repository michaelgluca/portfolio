About

From the frontline to the Power Platform.

I own Power Platform solutions end to end in the UK public sector: discovery and design through to deployment, governance and continuous improvement. That means the whole platform, not one corner of it: canvas and model-driven apps, cloud and desktop flows, Power BI models, Dataverse data design and security, and the environment, DLP and ALM administration underneath.

Before that I spent years on the frontline of the services I now build for, most recently leading a team of enforcement officers, and completed an MSc in Software Engineering alongside the day job. I understand the operational pressure, the compliance requirements, and what it takes for a solution to work in the public sector.

Beyond the platform I build for the web and iOS (Swift, SwiftUI), and I care about the engineering side of low-code: proper ALM, CI/CD through Azure DevOps, and solutions the next person can maintain.

Copilot Studio

AI Builder
